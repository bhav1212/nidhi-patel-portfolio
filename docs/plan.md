## 1. Positioning

Nidhi Patel is an early-career data scientist and AI engineer in Frankfurt am Main, open to relocation, whose evidence is employer work at Miles & More (Lufthansa Group), Barkow Consulting, Vonovia and Amprion, three Databricks credentials and three public artefacts. This site is written for hiring managers and technical screeners in Frankfurt and the wider DACH market filling Data Scientist, Machine Learning Engineer, AI Engineer and Data Engineer roles. In thirty seconds it must prove three things: that she has done data and ML work in operational settings for more than four years across working-student, internship and full-time contracts; that she can build and explain both an LLM/RAG system and a Databricks-based pipeline rather than only notebook models; and that her claims are checkable through named employers, credential IDs and public repositories or reports. It must not imply a seniority, production ownership, team size, client identity, availability date or quantitative business result that the CV cannot support.

## 5. Design system

### 5.1 Type

| Role | Family | Size / line-height / tracking | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display, H1, H2, pull quote | Fraunces | `clamp(2.75rem, 1.8rem + 4vw, 4rem)` / 1.02 / −0.03em for display; H2 `clamp(1.875rem, 1.5rem + 1.5vw, 2.25rem)` / 1.2 / −0.02em | 400 or 500 roman; 400 italic for one emphasis span only |
| H3, body, UI | Geist | H3 `clamp(1.25rem, 1.15rem + 0.5vw, 1.5rem)` / 1.3 / −0.01em; body `clamp(1.0625rem, 1rem + 0.25vw, 1.125rem)` / 1.6 / 0 | 400 body, 500 UI and labels |
| Metadata, labels, IDs, dates | JetBrains Mono | 0.75rem / 1.4 / +0.06em; small 0.875rem / 1.45 / +0.01em | 400 or 500 |

Prose measure 65ch; headings `text-wrap: balance`; paragraphs `text-wrap: pretty`; metrics `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`. No justified text, no all-caps paragraphs, no mono body copy. Fraunces ships as **400 roman, 400 italic and 500 roman**; Geist as a variable 400–500; JetBrains Mono as 400–500. Latin subset only. Estimate **120–145KB total**, hard CI failure above **150KB**, actual `stat -c %s` recorded in the font budget table. Preload Fraunces roman and Geist roman only. If the budget fails, drop the Fraunces italic file and use roman emphasis rather than a faux italic. Font files are fetched from the upstream OFL releases, not copied from the reference snapshot; the existing `public/assets/fonts/` set is provisional and re-sourced at build.

### 5.2 Colour tokens

The light paper is warmed to `#FAF7F0` so it is not a near-copy of the reference's `#fafaf7`; the structural move still carries distinctiveness. Contrast measured with the WCAG 2.x relative-luminance formula on 21 November 2026 and re-tested in the token test at build.

| Token | Light | Contrast | Dark | Contrast |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `--paper` | `#FAF7F0` | ground | `#0E1211` | ground |
| `--surface` | `#F1EDE3` | cards and plates | `#161B1A` | cards and plates |
| `--ink` | `#171A1C` | 16.34:1 paper / 14.96:1 surface | `#EDEFEA` | 16.29:1 paper |
| `--muted` | `#565F5E` | 6.14:1 paper / 5.62:1 surface | `#A2ABA7` | 8.01:1 paper / 7.4:1 surface |
| `--rule` | `#D9D5CA` | 1.37:1, decorative only | `#2A3230` | decorative only |
| `--line` | `#87847A` | 3.50:1 paper / 3.20:1 surface | `#636D6A` | 3.53:1 paper / 3.26:1 surface |
| `--accent` | `#0E5F57` | 7.03:1 paper / 6.43:1 surface | `#5FD3BE` | 10.37:1 paper / 9.6:1 surface |
| `--accent-ink` | `#FFFFFF` | 7.52:1 on accent | `#08110F` | 10.52:1 on accent |
| `--focus` | `#0E5F57` | 7.03:1 paper | `#5FD3BE` | 10.37:1 paper |

`--rule` is decorative and must never bound a control. `--line` is the functional boundary and clears 3:1 with a thin margin; it is recorded in the token test, not left to prose. One accent hue, target 90% neutral / 7% accent / 3% secondary. Above the fold there are **two accent surfaces**: the primary CTA and the focus ring; the inline hero link is ink-underlined, not accent-coloured, so the "exactly twice" claim is true by construction.

Focus recipe for both themes: 2px `--paper` inner ring plus 2px `--accent` outer ring, 2px offset; verify against the sticky header at 200% zoom. Prose links are underlined; never colour alone.

Acceptance target is WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 body text, 3:1 large text, UI boundaries and graphical objects; focus visible at ≥3:1 against both component and ground; 24×24px minimum targets, aiming for 44×44; 200% text resize and 400% zoom without loss; 320px reflow; no keyboard traps.

### 5.3 Data ramp

The brand accent is not a categorical series. Light series: `#0E5F57` (7.03:1 paper), `#35506B` (7.82:1), `#A16207` (4.60:1), `#9A4A3A` (5.75:1). Dark series: `#7FD8C8` (10.42:1 on dark surface), `#9DB7D6` (8.44:1), `#D9A441` (7.74:1), `#D98B7A` (6.57:1). Every series is direct-labelled and carries a second encoding (dash pattern or marker shape), because the light 3/4 pair and the dark 1/2 pair converge under deuteranopia even when luminance passes.

Sequential ramps are separate per theme: light `#6F9187` to `#174A43` (low end 3.01:1 on light surface); dark `#3F6F65` to `#9FE3D2` (low end 3.05:1 on dark surface). The review proposed `#8FB8AC`, but that measures 2.04:1 on the light paper, so the ramp starts one step darker. Five bands maximum, every band bounded by a 1px `--line` hairline, direct labels in `--ink`, and no chart text in a series colour. Acceptance is a CVD simulation plus the rendered chart, not a hue-wheel argument.

### 5.4 Spacing, layout and radii

4px base scale: 0.25 / 0.5 / 0.75 / 1 / 1.5 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 6rem. Container max 1200px; 12-column desktop grid; gutter `clamp(1.25rem, 4vw, 2rem)`; section rhythm `clamp(4rem, 8vw, 7rem)`; single column below 768px. Breakpoints: 640, 768, 1024, 1280, mobile-first.

Ledger columns at 1440: Period 2, System 3, Decision 4, Evidence 2, Route 1. At 1024: 2 / 3 / 3 / 3 / 1. At 768 each row becomes two lines: period and system on line one; decision on line two; evidence and route right-aligned. At 390 the row becomes a stacked definition list with the route as the full-width link. The Experience timeline keeps a fixed **5.5rem date column** until 768, where dates move above the role. This is the literal replacement for the undefined three-card 7/5 layout.

Radii: `--r-xs` 2px for chips and tags; `--r-sm` 4px for inputs; `--r-md` 8px for cards and figures; `--r-lg` 12px for the evidence panel and contact plane; `--r-pill` 999px for badges only. No shadows; borders and surface steps carry hierarchy. One full-bleed hairline rule may separate the hero from the ledger, with the section numeral sitting on it.

### 5.5 Motion budget

One signature moment per page: the ledger rows reveal once as a group on first entry — 12px translate plus opacity, 400–500ms, 40–60ms stagger, maximum six items. Everything else is micro-interaction.

| Surface | Allowed | Budget |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | None; the H1 is static and is the LCP element | 0ms |
| Section reveals | Opacity plus 12–16px translate, once, 3–5 groups per page | 400–500ms |
| Ledger rows | The signature stagger, once | 400–500ms plus stagger |
| Rows and cards on hover/focus | 2px background step plus border colour | 150–200ms |
| Figures | One draw or fade per figure | 600–900ms |
| Theme change | Colour transition | 150ms |
| Micro UI | Press, menu, badge state | 120–180ms |

`transform` and `opacity` only; no bounce or elastic easing. Reduced motion means **no trigger exists**, not a faster animation: the final frame is present in HTML, no stylesheet sets `opacity: 0`, and the `has-js` class gates any pre-hide. No count-up anywhere; every number is in the DOM at first paint.

### 5.6 Icons and SVG

Inline SVG only, no icon font and no third-party sprite. A single `Icon` primitive renders 24×24 with 1.5px `currentColor` strokes, `aria-hidden="true"` when decorative and a labelled `<title>` when meaningful. Architecture diagrams are hand-authored SVG with consistent 1px and 1.5px strokes, HTML labels for text (so they inherit type and zoom), a visible "conceptual" note where redrawn, and real `alt` text or a linked long description. No stock imagery, no screenshots of internal dashboards, no generated "AI brain" art.

## 8. Three deliberate structural differentiators

**D1 — The evidence ledger replaces the bento and the stat strip.** Selected work is a five-column index: `PERIOD / SYSTEM / DECISION IT ENCODES / EVIDENCE / ROUTE`. The Evidence column carries the four-state badge, so the site's biggest constraint — no quantitative outcomes, one method-only project, confidential employer work — becomes the visual system rather than a disclaimer. It reads as something a data person would build, and it fixes the flagship-width, undefined-grid and thin-rail problems in one move.

**D2 — One chronology rail runs through ledger and Experience.** The same mono period column and hairline rhythm continue from work rows into role rows, so each project sits beside the role that produced it and the page reads as a dated document, not a stack of cards. The rail is a single shared grid, not a second decorative timeline; it is why the proof strip can be deleted without losing chronology.

**D3 — About is recomposed as one measured paragraph plus a three-row "How I work" list.** The three rows are modelling, delivery and evidence, each one sentence. This removes the reference's most recognisable composition — prose left, facts aside — without adding a new section or a second facts panel. The sixth capability cluster, Languages, closes the preceding grid as a four-item definition list; About mentions the working pair once, without repeating the list.
